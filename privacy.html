Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

GreenBin lets people report waste problems in their community. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never store your actual password.
  • Reports you submit: the location or landmark you type, category, description, urgency, an optional nickname, an optional photo, and an optional map pin (latitude and longitude).
  • Sign-in cookie: a cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.
  • Security records: your network address (IP) is briefly used to limit repeated sign-in attempts. These records are deleted within a day.

What is public

Your reports, including the location, description, nickname, photo and map pin, are visible to other users of GreenBin. Your email address is never shown to other users. Please do not include names, phone numbers or your home address in a report.

Your location

GreenBin only reads your device location if you tap "Use my current location". It is used only to place the pin on your report.

How we use your information

To run the app: sign you in, show reports and the community map, and let administrators mark reports as collected. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and stores its database.
  • OpenStreetMap provides map images. Your device contacts their servers to load the map.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app (Account tab → Delete account) or on the web at greenbin-prototype.pages.dev/delete-account.html. This permanently deletes your account, your reports and your photos.

Children

GreenBin is not intended for children under 13.
