Waste-sorting tips

Quick guidance to sort correctly and keep recyclables clean. General advice for the prototype — always follow your local council rules.

♳ Plastic

Rinse bottles and containers. Flatten to save space. No plastic bags or film in the recycling bin.

🍂 Organic

Bag food scraps and keep compost bins closed. No liquids or plastics in compost. Put out on compost day.

📰 Paper

Keep paper dry and flat. Shredded paper goes in a paper bag. Greasy pizza boxes go in organic/general.

🫙 Glass

Rinse jars and bottles. No broken glass loose — wrap and label it. Never put light bulbs in glass recycling.

🔩 Metal

Rinse cans and tins. Empty aerosols completely. Scrap metal goes to a collection point, not the kerb bin.

🔋 E-waste & Hazardous

Never bin batteries, paint, or chemicals. Take them to a designated drop-off point and keep them dry and separate.
